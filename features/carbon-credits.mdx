Carbonkrishi's SOC (Soil Organic Carbon) credit estimator quantifies how much carbon FYM and compost inputs lock into the soil over a cropping season. It applies a configurable **permanence buffer** — defaulting to 20% — to account for reversal risk, then translates the net sequestered carbon into a monetary value range using India's **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)** price band of ₹600–₹900 per tonne CO₂-eq.

This estimate gives rice farmers a concrete INR figure to compare against the cost premium of organic inputs, enabling data-driven decisions about whether to invest in an organic nutrient management strategy.

---

## How SOC Is Calculated

Carbon sequestration is estimated from the physical and chemical properties of each organic amendment:

| Parameter | Farm Yard Manure | Compost |
|---|---|---|
| Dry Matter (%) | 25% | 55% |
| Carbon Content (%) | 25% | 25% |
| Humification Coefficient | 0.25 | 0.35 |

The humification coefficient represents the fraction of organic carbon that stabilises into humus rather than being mineralised back to CO₂.

**SOC formula** (applied separately to FYM and compost):

```text
SOC (t CO₂-eq) = (input_kg × DM% × C% × H%) / 1000 × 3.667
```

Where `3.667` is the C → CO₂ molecular weight conversion factor (44/12).

**Example for 10,000 kg FYM:**

```text
SOC = (10000 × 0.25 × 0.25 × 0.25) / 1000 × 3.667
    = (156.25 / 1000) × 3.667
    = 0.5730 t CO₂-eq/ha
```

### Permanence Buffer

A buffer percentage (default **20%**, range 0–100%) is withheld from the tradeable credit total to account for the risk that sequestered carbon may be re-emitted (e.g., if the farmer stops applying amendments or tills deeply):

```text
credits_tco2 = soc_before_buffer_tco2 × (1 - buffer_pct / 100)
buffer_withheld_tco2 = soc_before_buffer_tco2 - credits_tco2
```

## Example Request

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/credits/soc \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"manure": 10000, "compost": 1500, "buffer_pct": 20}'
```

**Example response:**

```json
{
  "fym_soc_tco2": 0.5730,
  "compost_soc_tco2": 0.2869,
  "fym_credits_tco2": 0.4584,
  "compost_credits_tco2": 0.2295,
  "soc_before_buffer_tco2": 0.8599,
  "credits_tco2": 0.6879,
  "buffer_withheld_tco2": 0.1720,
  "value_low_inr": 412.74,
  "value_high_inr": 619.11
}
```

<ResponseField name="fym_soc_tco2" type="float">
  Gross SOC sequestered from the FYM input before buffer, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="compost_soc_tco2" type="float">
  Gross SOC sequestered from the compost input before buffer, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="fym_credits_tco2" type="float">
  Tradeable credits from FYM after applying the buffer, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="compost_credits_tco2" type="float">
  Tradeable credits from compost after applying the buffer, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="soc_before_buffer_tco2" type="float">
  Total gross SOC from all amendments before the permanence buffer is applied, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="credits_tco2" type="float">
  Net tradeable carbon credits after deducting the buffer, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="buffer_withheld_tco2" type="float">
  Amount withheld in the permanence buffer pool, in t CO₂-eq/ha.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="value_low_inr" type="float">
  Monetary value of credits at the CCTS floor price of ₹600/t CO₂-eq.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="value_high_inr" type="float">
  Monetary value of credits at the CCTS ceiling price of ₹900/t CO₂-eq.
</ResponseField>

## India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)

India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, notified by MoEFCC and administered through BEE (Bureau of Energy Efficiency), establishes a domestic market for verified carbon reductions and removals. Agricultural soil carbon sequestration is an eligible activity under the scheme.

**Current indicative CCTS price band: ₹600–₹900 per tonne CO₂-eq.**

### Registering Your Credits

To monetise SOC credits, farmers need:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Maintain a 3-season input log">
    Record FYM and compost quantities applied per season with dates, quantities, and source supplier details. Photos and GPS-tagged field records strengthen the claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct baseline soil tests">
    A soil organic carbon baseline test at accredited labs (ICAR / KVK) is required before the first crediting period begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage a verified aggregator">
    Indian farmers can register through aggregators operating under NCDEX (domestic CCTS), Verra (VM0042 — Improved Agricultural Land Management), or Gold Standard (AWD Methane Reduction). Aggregators handle MRV (Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification) on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim and trade credits">
    After third-party verification at the end of the crediting period, carbon credits are issued to your account and can be traded on the Indian Carbon Market or international registries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Carbonkrishi SOC estimate is an **indicative screening tool** based on standard humification coefficients. Actual issuable credits depend on the MRV methodology adopted by your chosen aggregator, baseline soil OC levels, and additionality requirements. Always confirm with a registered carbon project developer before committing to an input strategy for credit generation.
</Note>
